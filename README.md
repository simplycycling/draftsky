# DraftSky

A Bluesky client built around smarter posting.

## What it is

DraftSky is a web-based Bluesky client that lets you create posting templates — named presets that automatically append hashtags and other recurring text to your posts. If you post about the same topics regularly, you shouldn't have to type the same hashtags every time.

Beyond posting, DraftSky shows you your Following feed and — immediately after you post — automatically surfaces a merged feed of the hashtags you just used, so you can see the conversation you've just joined.

## What it's trying to be

Most Bluesky clients are built around reading. DraftSky is built around posting with intent — for people who post about specific communities, sports, hobbies, or topics consistently and want the friction of repetitive hashtag entry out of the way.

The goal is a client that feels like it knows what you post about, and gets out of your way so you can actually post it.
