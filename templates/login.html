DraftSky

A Bluesky client built around smarter posting

Free Forever - Premium Coming Soon

DraftSky is a full Bluesky client built around template-based posting. Save the hashtag sets and recurring text you use for a topic once, then pick a template when you compose - it's appended to your post automatically, so you never retype the same tags again.

The moment you post, DraftSky switches you into a live, merged feed of every hashtag you just used - so you land straight in the conversation you've entered.

Posting templates

Named sets of hashtags and recurring text, appended to your post with correct facets - no more retyping.

The feed follows your post

Post, and DraftSky drops you into a merged, recency-sorted feed of every hashtag you used.

A complete client

Feeds, threads, replies, quotes, profiles and notifications - everything you expect, in one place.
