DraftSky

Where DraftSky is headed

Shipped

DraftSky is already a full Bluesky client. Here's what's live today.

  • Template-based posting with correct hashtag and mention facets
  • Post, then land straight in a merged feed of every hashtag you used
  • Saved feed tabs from your Bluesky pinned feeds
  • Threads, replies, quotes, and reposts
  • Likes
  • Profiles
  • Notifications with a live unread badge
  • @-mention typeahead in the composer
  • Inline video playback
  • Image, link, and GIF-link embeds
  • Your Bluesky mutes and blocks inherited automatically
  • A free tier that's free forever
